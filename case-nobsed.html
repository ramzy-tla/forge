The Challenge

Look as established online as they are on site.

NOBSED had 16+ years of work and 100+ projects, but no digital home to prove it. They needed a site that built instant trust, showcased their full range of services, and turned visitors into enquiries — without looking like a template.

Our Approach

Brand-led design, engineered to convert.

We built a fast, mobile-first multi-page site around their navy & gold identity — a clear service catalogue, a filterable project portfolio, and a smart contact funnel that pre-fills enquiries by service.

What We Built

Everything they needed to win work online

Multi-page website

Home, About, Services, Projects, Contact — plus Privacy & Terms — on one clean, fast design system.

12-service catalogue

Every plumbing, civil and construction service laid out with its own imagery and a one-tap request flow.

Filterable portfolio

100+ real project photos, sorted by quality, filterable by category — proof that scrolls.

Smart contact funnel

Service cards pre-fill the enquiry form, so leads arrive ready to quote — wired to email, no backend.

Floating contact widget

Call, WhatsApp, SMS and enquiry in one tap, on every page and device.

Social-share system

Branded preview cards so every shared link looks professional on WhatsApp and socials.
